1. Enquiries and bookings

Submitting the website form or adding items to an enquiry cart is a request, not a confirmed hire. A booking is confirmed only when we accept it in writing (usually by email) and any required payment or bond arrangements are agreed.

Prices on the site are in Australian dollars and may change until your booking is confirmed. We’ll confirm availability, totals, pickup or delivery, and any extras before you pay.

2. Hire period

Standard hire is a flexible 4-day period unless we agree otherwise. Tell us your event dates on enquiry so we can set pickup and return times that work.

3. What’s included

  • Film and Instant camera hires include the film stated on the product (colour rolls or Instant packs).

4. Bond and security hold

A $70 refundable bond applies per hired camera and for the Vintage Video Guestbook, unless we confirm a different amount. The bond is returned when gear comes back on time, complete, and in the same condition (fair wear and tear excepted).

The security hold is based on replacement value. If repair or replacement costs more than the hold, we may invoice the difference. If it’s less, we only capture what we need.

5. Pickup, delivery and return

  • Brisbane studio pickup is free.
  • Local delivery starts from about $18 depending on kit size — we’ll confirm with your enquiry.

You’re responsible for the gear from pickup or delivery until we receive it back. Please follow any care notes we send with the kit.

Film results depend on how the camera is used, lighting, and the film itself. We don’t guarantee how guest photos will turn out. If you add develop & scan or a print pack, we’ll organise that after the camera is returned. Typical colour develop & high-res scan is about 4 business days after we receive the film; prints usually add another 2–3 business days. Rush, B&W and slide options are on enquiry. Lab work is subject to the lab’s process; we’ll pass on any issues we’re told about.
