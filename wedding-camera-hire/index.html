How wedding hire works

  1. 01

    Book

    Choose your cameras or wedding kit and send an enquiry through our website. Tell us your guest count and we’ll help you choose the right kit.

  2. 02

    Shoot

    Collect from our pickup location just outside Brisbane CBD, or have them delivered before the day (extra fees apply).

  3. 03

    Return

    Before you return, you can export the photos yourself or ask us to do it for you. Then return the kit back to us — we’ll check everything’s all good and refund the bond on the spot.
