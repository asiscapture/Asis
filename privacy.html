Privacy policy

Last updated 10 August 2026

unposed (Brisbane, QLD 4000, Australia) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and store personal information when you use asiscapture.com.au or contact us. Email: asis.capture@gmail.com.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, where they apply.

1. What we collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number and email address
  • Hire start and end dates, event type, and message details
  • Items you’re enquiring about (cameras, bundles, keepsakes, add-ons)
  • Pickup, delivery or drop-box preferences
  • Payment and bond details if we take those for a confirmed booking
  • Photos or video you choose to share with us after a hire

You don’t have to give us everything on the enquiry form — name is required to reply; other fields are optional. If you skip contact details, we may not be able to complete a booking.

2. How we collect it

  • The hire enquiry form on this website
  • Email, phone or messages you send us
  • Your browser, for the enquiry cart (see cookies below)

The enquiry form is sent through Web3Forms so it reaches our inbox.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and confirm availability
  • To arrange pickup, delivery, bonds, returns and add-ons
  • To organise developing, scans or prints if you request them
  • To improve our website and hire process
  • To meet legal, tax or insurance requirements

We don’t sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We only share what’s needed to run the hire, for example:

  • Web3Forms, to deliver enquiry emails
  • A film lab, if you ask us to develop, scan or print
  • Delivery partners, if we deliver or collect a kit
  • Payment providers, if you pay us electronically
  • Professional advisers or insurers, if required
  • Authorities, if the law requires it

5. Cookies and the enquiry cart

This site uses your browser’s local storage to remember enquiry cart items on this device so you can continue on another page. That stays on your device until you clear site data or remove the items. We don’t use advertising cookies or sell browsing data.

6. Photos and guest content

Film scans, Instant prints and video guestbook recordings from your hire are your memories. We only use images or clips on our website or social media if you clearly agree. Guest photos on cameras or in guestbooks are not collected by us unless you send them to us.

7. Storage and security

We keep enquiry and booking details only as long as needed for the hire, follow-up, and any legal or accounting reasons, then delete or de-identify them where practical. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no email or website is completely secure.

8. Overseas disclosure

Some tools (including Web3Forms) may process data on servers outside Australia. If that happens, we’ll still take reasonable steps to keep your information protected.

9. Access, correction and complaints

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or make a privacy complaint by emailing asis.capture@gmail.com. We’ll respond as soon as we reasonably can. If you’re not satisfied, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

10. Children

Our hires are aimed at adults booking for events. If you believe we’ve collected information about a child without a parent or guardian’s involvement, please contact us and we’ll delete it where appropriate.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
