Party hire FAQs

Is film included?

Yes — every film and Instant camera hire includes colour rolls or Instant packs in the listed price. The Party Camera Kit also includes two extra Mini packs so the night lasts longer.

What about the bond?

A $70 refundable bond applies per camera. It’s returned when gear comes back on time and undamaged.

How does pickup and delivery work?

Brisbane pickup is free. Local delivery starts from about $18 depending on kit size — we’ll confirm with your enquiry. Returns can go to the studio or our 24-hour drop box.
