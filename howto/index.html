How to hire

How camera hire works

Three simple steps — then you're ready to shoot.

  1. 01

    Book

    Choose your cameras or bundle and send an enquiry through our website. Standard 3-day hire — pick up the day before your event and return the day after.

  2. 02

    Shoot

    Collect from our pickup location just outside Brisbane CBD, or have it delivered (extra fees apply).

  3. 03

    Return

    Before you return, you can export the photos yourself or ask us to do it for you. Then return the kit back to us — we'll check everything's all good and refund the bond on the spot.

Film is included in every film and Instant hire.

We can load the film for you, and handle develop & scans too (extra fees apply).
