Camera hire FAQs

Quick answers before you enquire.

How long is a hire?

Standard hire is 3 days — pick up the day before your event and return the day after. Need longer, shorter, or different dates? Tell us what you need in your enquiry and we’ll see what we can do.

Is film, developing and scanning included?

Film is included with every film and Instant camera hire — colour rolls and Instant packs are part of the listed price. Need more? Ask when you enquire. Develop & scan for film cameras is available as an add-on — tell us in your enquiry.

What about the bond?

A $50 refundable bond applies per camera (and for the video guestbook) to cover damages and late fees. It’s returned when gear comes back on time and undamaged.

How does pickup and delivery work?

Collect from our pickup location just outside Brisbane CBD, or arrange local delivery (extra fees apply). Then return the kit — we’ll check everything’s all good and refund the bond on the spot.

Who exports the digital photos?

Before you return, you can export the photos yourself or ask us to do it for you.
