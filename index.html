Brand story

True to the moment.

We started unposed in Brisbane because too many of the weddings, weekends and nights we loved only lived on a phone — scrolled past, forgotten, and rarely printed. The photos that stayed with us were the ones we could hold: printed, imperfect and unposed, but made to last.

We want everyone to experience that feeling — a photo in your hands, a moment you can keep, and memories that live beyond your camera roll. So we make film, instant and digicam hire simple. Pass a camera around. Capture the moment. Keep the print. Let the album stay long after the day is over.

From our studio in Brisbane, we're here to help you capture life as it happens — unposed.

Camera hire FAQs

Quick answers before you enquire.

How long is a hire?

Standard hire is 3 days — pick up the day before your event and return the day after. Tell us your dates in the enquiry form.

Is film included?

Yes on every film and Instant camera hire — colour rolls and Instant packs are part of the listed price. Digital cameras include a 32GB SD card instead.

What about the bond and postage?

A $50 refundable bond applies per camera (and for the video guestbook) to cover damages and late fees. It’s returned when gear comes back on time and undamaged. Delivery is available (extra fees apply) — we’ll confirm with your enquiry.

How does pickup and delivery work?

Collect from our pickup location just outside Brisbane CBD, or arrange local delivery (extra fees apply). Before you return, you can export the photos yourself or ask us to do it for you. Then return the kit — we’ll check everything’s all good and refund the bond on the spot.

Who exports the digital photos?

Before you return, you can export the photos yourself or ask us to do it for you.
