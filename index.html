Camera hire FAQs

Quick answers before you enquire.

How long is a hire?

Flexible 4-day hire periods as standard. Tell us your dates in the enquiry form.

Is film included?

Yes on every film and Instant camera hire — colour rolls and Instant packs are part of the listed price. Digital cameras include a 32GB SD card instead. Extra film is available as an add-on if you need more shots.

What about the bond and postage?

A $70 refundable bond applies per camera (and for the video guestbook). It’s returned when gear comes back on time and undamaged. Brisbane pickup is free; local delivery starts from about $18 depending on kit size — we’ll confirm delivery with your enquiry.

How does pickup and delivery work?

Brisbane pickup is free. We also offer local delivery and a convenient 24-hour return drop box — tell us what suits when you enquire.

Can you load film and develop my photos?

Yes. Load film yourself or let us prepare it. After return we can organise developing, scans and print packs.

What can I hire?

Digital, film and Instant cameras, keepsakes and occasion bundles — browse by category or bundle to build your enquiry.
