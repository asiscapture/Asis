Our story

True to the moment.

We started Livis in Brisbane because too many of the weddings, weekends and nights we loved only lived on a phone — scrolled past, forgotten, and rarely printed. The photos that stayed with us were the ones we could hold: printed, imperfect and candid, but made to last.

We want everyone to experience that feeling — a photo in your hands, a moment you can keep, and memories that live beyond your camera roll. So we make film, instant and digicam hire simple. Pass a camera around. Capture the moment. Keep the print. Let the album stay long after the day is over.

From our studio in Brisbane, we're here to help you capture life as it happens — as is.
