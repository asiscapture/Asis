Wedding hire FAQs

Is film included?

Yes — every film and Instant camera hire includes colour rolls or Instant packs in the listed price. Extra Mini and Square packs are available as add-ons if you need more shots for a bigger guest list.

What about the bond?

A $70 refundable bond applies per camera and for the Vintage Video Guestbook. It’s returned when gear comes back on time and undamaged.

How does pickup and delivery work?

Brisbane pickup is free. Local delivery starts from about $18 depending on kit size — we’ll confirm with your enquiry. Returns can go to the studio or our 24-hour drop box.
