Wedding hire FAQs

Is film included?

Yes — every film and Instant camera hire includes colour rolls or Instant packs in the listed price. Need more for a bigger guest list? Tell us when you enquire.

What about the bond?

A $50 refundable bond applies per camera and for the Vintage Video Guestbook to cover damages and late fees. It’s returned when gear comes back on time and undamaged.

How does pickup and delivery work?

Collect from our pickup location just outside Brisbane CBD, or arrange local delivery (extra fees apply). Before you return, you can export the photos yourself or ask us to do it for you. Then return the kit — we’ll check everything’s all good and refund the bond on the spot.
