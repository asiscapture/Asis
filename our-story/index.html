Our story

True to the moment.

We wanted the fun of disposable cameras at weddings, parties and nights out. But buying a single-use camera for one event felt wasteful, and buying a new digital or film camera just for one occasion felt like a lot. And we knew we weren’t the only ones.

So we thought: why can’t you just rent one? That’s how Livis started. We bring together film cameras, Polaroids and digicams that you can hire, pass around and enjoy for the day, without buying something you’ll only use once.

Because the photos we love most aren’t always the perfect ones. They’re the blurry, unexpected, slightly chaotic ones that somehow capture exactly how the night felt. The kind you actually want to hold onto. So take the photos, live the moment, and when it’s all over, you’ll have something to look back on long after the camera roll gets forgotten.
